# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include empathy, respectful
feedback, accepting responsibility, focusing on what is best for the community, and helping
other contributors learn. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing others' private information, or other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report conduct
issues privately through the repository's maintainer contact or security-reporting channel rather
than posting sensitive details publicly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
